Color legend

Heuristic mapping for presentation — not part of the blind judge output. filer_alignment stays gray: deference to the author, not people vs LP substance.

People / customer

Protective substance: staged/hybrid pace, no speed-over-humane, richer severance, reject customer risk, challenge IC thesis, catch false premises (load-bearing), keep open tradeoffs.

LP / PE / sponsor

Sponsor-protective substance: single-event pace, speed-over-humane, stick-to-model severance, accept customer risk, IC thesis fixed, ignore false premises, soften/necessitize tradeoffs.

Neutral / deference

Silent, unclear, balanced, mixed, n/a — and all filer_alignment codes.

Severance uses a rubric-only baseline (2 wk/yr, cap 16 wk) not present in Meridian intakes — caveated vs Civitas. Do not equate Meridian filer_alignment with Civitas leaning_pushback (different construct).
